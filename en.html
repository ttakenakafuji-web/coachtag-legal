Not in your average editor

Things you won't find in a regular video editor.

◎
Pro

Auto-tracking

AI tracks the player you tap, even as the camera moves. No more redrawing frame by frame. A feature usually found only in professional analysis software, built right into the app.

◉
Pro

Drawing that doesn't cover players

CoachTag detects the pitch color and automatically masks drawings so they never sit on top of players or the ball. Zones and lines pass underneath the players for a broadcast-style look, and exported video looks exactly the same.

▣
Pro

Ghost

Cut out a player and move them to where they should be. The player at the original position disappears naturally, so you can show positioning free from what actually happened.

◫

Second angle

Sync two camera angles to review and export them side by side. Choose one angle, mix both, or mute the audio.

⌘

Designed for privacy

Your video is never uploaded to a server. Tagging, drawing and exporting all happen on your device. The App Store privacy label says "Data Not Collected".

Workflow

Tagging, tactical drawing, export.
Your post-match workflow, in one app.

01

Tagging

Tag scenes with one tap while the video plays: good and bad moments in attack and defense, set pieces and more. Adjust the start and end timing (lead and lag time) to fit how you tag.

AT GoodAT BadDF GoodDF Bad
02

Tactical drawing

Draw tactics on tagged scenes with arrows, lines, zones, circles, text and vision cones. Animate them with keyframes, and burn explanations into a freeze frame.

03

Playlists

Collect tags into playlists and see them all on the tag board. A single playlist can span several match packages.

04

Export and share

Export a playlist as one video or as separate clips, then send them straight to players and staff with AirDrop or the Files app.
