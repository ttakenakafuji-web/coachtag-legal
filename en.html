Features

Turn match footage into clear coaching video.

Tag in one tap as you watch

Record good and bad moments in attack and defense, set pieces and more without pausing. Layer flags (press, win ball, shot and more) to sort every scene.

Tap a player to draw tactics

Connect, Spotlight, arrows, text and zones. CoachTag detects the pitch color so drawings sit at the players' feet.

AI tracks players for you

The disc follows the player even as the camera moves. Use Ghost to move a player to where they should be.

Tags × flags: trends at a glance

Click a cell in the table to select the matching scenes and turn them into a playlist.

Everything you need for match analysis.

Vision

Show where a player is looking with a cone from their feet or eye level.

Lofted and curling balls

Arrows that combine height and curve, with a shadow on the ground.

Freeze frames and keyframes

Pause to explain, and animate effects over time.

Playlists

Collect clips across matches and see them all on the tag board.

Second angle

Sync two cameras to review and export side by side.

Export and share

Clips with drawings burned in, to AirDrop, Photos or Files.
