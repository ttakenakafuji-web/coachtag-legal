CoachTag Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

CoachTag ("the App") is a Mac and iPad app for football video analysis, tagging and tactical drawing. The App never sends or stores your videos or analysis data on an external server. All data stays on your device (and in any location you choose yourself, such as iCloud Drive).

Information we collect

The App does not collect or send any personal information to a server of its own. Specifically:

  • Analysis data such as videos, tags and drawings: saved as files on your device only, and never sent outside the App (to a server or elsewhere).
  • Auto-tracking (AI): player detection and tracking run entirely on your device using a model bundled with the App. Video is never sent anywhere.
  • Subscription (purchase) information: purchases, restores and expiration checks are handled on your device through Apple's App Store. The App does not send receipts to Apple or any third party on its own. Payment itself is processed by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to the information it handles.
  • Analytics, ads and tracking: the App contains no analytics SDKs, ad SDKs or tracking.

Crash reports

If the App quits unexpectedly, macOS or iPadOS may create a diagnostic report. This is a standard operating-system feature, and the report is shared with Apple and the developer only if you have turned on sharing analytics with Apple and app developers. The App itself does not collect or send this information.

Sharing with third parties

Because the App sends no information outside your device, no information is provided to or shared with third parties.

Managing or canceling your subscription

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on iPad, or in System Settings > Apple ID > Media & Purchases > Subscriptions on Mac.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact information on the support page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App's features change. If there are important changes, we will announce them on this page.
