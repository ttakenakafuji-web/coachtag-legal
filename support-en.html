FAQ

  • Which plan should I choose?
    Choose Basic if you only need tagging, playlists and export, or Pro if you also want effects such as tactical drawing and auto-tracking. Both plans work on Mac and iPad, and your first month is free.
  • I subscribed on another device
    Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen in the app.
  • How do I cancel my subscription?
    On iPad, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Mac, go to System Settings > Apple ID > Media & Purchases > Subscriptions. You can cancel anytime.
  • How do I move data between Mac and iPad?
    Share the package (.ccpkg) via iCloud Drive, the Files app or AirDrop, and open it on either device.
  • How do I change the language?
    Use "表示言語 / Language" at the top of "⚙️ Tag Settings" (日本語, English, 한국어, Português, 简体中文).
  • Auto-tracking drifts
    Drag the disc to the right spot on that frame and press "Retrack Around Here". The correction stays on the timeline as ◆, and only the span around it is retracked.
